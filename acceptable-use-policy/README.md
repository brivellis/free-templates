# Acceptable Use Policy Template (free) — v1.0

A complete, editable acceptable use policy for organisations of any size, with a fictional worked example (Falcon Ridge IT Services) and an acknowledgement form.

## What's inside
- `Brivellis_Acceptable_Use_Policy_Template_v1.0.docx` — the policy (Word, Arial), 16 sections plus two annexes:
  - general rules, devices (organisation and personal / BYOD), email and messaging, internet and personal use, cloud services and file sharing, remote working;
  - **acceptable use of AI assistants**: approved tools only, no confidential data in public AI tools, human review of every output, labelling, an AI-use record, and no AI decisions about people, risk scores or evidence;
  - social media, monitoring and privacy notice, reporting, breaches, exceptions, review and approval;
  - Annex A acknowledgement form (the evidence an auditor asks for);
  - Annex B mapping to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A control numbers (A.5.10, A.6.2, A.8.1 and related) and NIST CSF 2.0 outcomes.
- Blue `[placeholders]` to replace, yellow guidance boxes and blue Falcon Ridge example boxes to delete before issue.

## How to use it
1. Replace every blue placeholder with your organisation's details.
2. Remove rules that do not fit how you work; keep every rule checkable.
3. Check sections 10, 11 and 13 (social media, monitoring, breaches) with HR and a legal adviser; rules differ by country.
4. Delete the guidance and example boxes, get top-management approval, and collect an acknowledgement from everyone in scope.
